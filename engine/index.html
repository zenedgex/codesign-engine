The problem

Chip design decisions are expensive to change.

The most important hardware decisions are often made early, when the least evidence is available. Architecture, memory, dataflow, precision and software mapping can take months to evaluate, and changing them later means significant engineering effort and costly design iterations.

Atelier brings these decisions into one intelligent design exploration loop.

01

Explore faster

Evaluate thousands of architecture and implementation possibilities instead of relying on a small number of manual experiments.

02

Optimize better

Find the best trade-offs across performance, power and area for your actual workloads, not for a generic benchmark.

03

Reduce design risk

Validate important design decisions earlier, before committing significant engineering resources to implementation.

What makes it different

Hardware and software, designed together.

A chip is only as good as the software that runs on it. Atelier evaluates hardware architectures together with their compiler mappings, so the architecture you select is not just efficient on paper. It is designed around how your workloads will actually execute.

Case study

YOLOv8n, end to end.

A full object detector, from its quantized model to a generated in-memory-compute accelerator: checked on real images, then a whole 640×640 frame run on the generated hardware.

0.364COCO mAP50-95 through the accelerator's integer arithmetic, on all 5,000 val2017 images (floating-point model: 0.371)
6 / 6detection outputs of a full 640×640 frame on the generated RTL, bit-identical to the integer reference
0.05%difference between the instant cycle estimate and RTL simulation over that frame (3.26 million cycles)
4 / 4blocks synthesized and floorplanned on ASAP7 7 nm with their memory macros, with timing reports: RISC-V core, compute engine, post-processing, activation unit

Model: Ultralytics YOLOv8n, INT8. Hardware: one 64×64 digital in-memory-compute engine with a RISC-V (CV32E40P) control core, simulated in Verilator. Implementation: synthesis and floorplan with OpenROAD-flow-scripts on the ASAP7 predictive kit. Next: your foundry. The RTL, constraints, memory list and physical-design kit carry over to your process design kit and memory compilers.
