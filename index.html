Results by workload

Proven on real workloads.

Give us the workload and the area, power and performance it must meet. Atelier searches architecture and compiler together and returns the design that meets every requirement, ranked by what you care about most, with the evidence for it.

LANGUAGE MODEL

Qwen3-0.6B, a whole token

All 645 matrix multiplies of a generated token, 28 layers and the output head, bit-exact on the generated RTL, with the logits equal to the integer reference; the cycle estimate within 0.004% of its 16.8 million cycles.

GEMM

Transformer matrix multiplies

Compiled through MLIR for 22 accelerator configurations, systolic and in-memory compute, from 4×4 to 128×64. Every program bit-exact against NumPy on the generated RTL; the cycle estimate is a median 1.4% from RTL simulation.

CONVOLUTION

CNN layers

All 63 convolution layers of YOLOv8n, from 3×3 stems to 1×1 heads, compiled onto in-memory-compute engines and run on the generated RTL, bit-exact with the integer reference.

NETWORK

YOLOv8n, end to end

The whole detector on chip: 0.364 mAP50-95 on COCO with the accelerator's integer arithmetic, a full 640×640 frame bit-exact on RTL, and the estimate within 0.05% of its 3.26 million cycles.
