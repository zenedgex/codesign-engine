How

Find the gaps, then prove them on their benchmark.

Four steps in Atelier Studio, on the accelerator as its vendor ships it.

01 / ANALYZE

Where the time goes

Layers measured on the RTL, set against the bound the hardware's description allows, and split: what the hardware needs, what it loses (design gaps), what its software adds (tool gaps).

02 / EXPLORE

Their design space

Every configuration the vendor's generator builds, on the vendor's own benchmark, with its own software's mapping replayed for each (exact against the RTL's DMA counters). The gaps are ranked parameter by parameter.

03 / PROPOSE

Beyond their generator

Where no parameter closes a gap, Atelier proposes the design dimension that would, and costs it: a depthwise mode, an activation table on the output path.

04 / MEASURE

Build and run

The pick is built with the vendor's generator and runs the vendor's benchmark unchanged on its RTL: same network, weights, batch and correctness check, its own cycle printout as the result.

Gemmini · UC Berkeley

MobileNet and ResNet-50, as Gemmini ships them.

Gemmini is an open-source systolic-array generator from UC Berkeley, part of Chipyard. Its own benchmarks are gemmini-rocc-tests' imagenet/mobilenet.c and resnet50.c: four images, int8, through its own library, gemmini.h. Stock design: GemminiRocketConfig, a 16×16 mesh, 256 KB scratchpad, 64 KB accumulator and 128-bit DMA at 500 MHz.

On the 19 layers measured on Gemmini's RTL, about 79% of the time is what its own software (gemmini.h) adds: rows padded to whole DMA transfers, outputs written in small requests, array passes it does not need. The hardware needs about 18%; it loses about 8% more (its writes, idle lanes, a scratchpad too small for some layers). Fitted split of measured time; the model is within 5% of the total. fitted

NVDLA · NVIDIA

Method

What every number stands on.

Measured on RTL: cycles from Verilator simulation of the RTL the vendor's generator built (Chipyard 1.15.0), with the benchmark's own check passing. Fitted: Atelier's cycle model of the vendor's software, checked on layers and configurations it was not fitted on. Predicted / estimate: the model before a build; area from ASAP7 7 nm macro and cell figures; proposed design dimensions until built. Running: measurements under way. Vendors' networks, weights and numerics are run unchanged; nothing is re-quantized.

Gemmini and NVDLA are named only to identify the open-source designs and benchmarks measured here; ZenEdgeX is not affiliated with or endorsed by UC Berkeley or NVIDIA.
