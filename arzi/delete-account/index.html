Delete your Arzi account

For the Arzi and Arzi for Advocates apps, by ZenEdgeX

In the app (instant)
  1. Open Arzi or Arzi for Advocates and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
If you have an upcoming consultation, cancel it or wait until it is over first.
By email
If you can no longer sign in, email hello@zenedgex.in from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Arzi account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, name, email and profile photo
  • Cases you track and the matters you described when booking
  • For advocates: your listing, fees, hours, free-call setting and payout account details

What is kept, and for how long

Records of paid consultations, payments, refunds and payouts (date, amount and transaction references) are kept without your name or matter for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, generally eight years, and then deleted.
