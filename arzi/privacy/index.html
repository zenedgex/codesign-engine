Privacy Policy

Applies to the Arzi and Arzi for Advocates apps · Last updated 30 October 2026

Arzi is a technology platform operated by ZenEdgeX (“we”, “us”). It provides free legal information tools and lets people book private video consultations with advocates enrolled with a State Bar Council. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

DataFromWhy
Email address or mobile number, and nameEveryone who signs inTo create your account, sign you in with a one-time code sent by email or SMS, and show your name to the other party of a consultation
Profile photo (optional)Anyone who adds oneShown on your account; for advocates, on your listing
Description of your legal matterClients, when bookingShared only with the advocate you book, so they can prepare

Camera and microphoneDuring a video call, with your permissionTo carry the live call. Calls are not recorded or stored

We do not collect your location, contacts or browsing history, and we do not show advertising or sell personal data.

Who we share it with

  • The other party of your consultation: your name, photo and, for clients, the matter you described.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Mumbai, India), Razorpay (payments, refunds and advocate payouts), LiveKit (live video, routed through servers in India) our email and SMS providers (sign-in codes). They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it.

Payment card and UPI details are entered on Razorpay’s pages and never reach us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Records of paid consultations, payments, refunds and payouts are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally eight years), without your name or matter once your account is deleted.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by database rules so that each person sees only their own records and the consultations they are part of.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your details in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account from Account → Delete account in either app, or see how to delete your account.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can turn off camera, microphone and notification permissions in your phone’s settings.
  • Grievances: contact our Grievance Officer below. We respond within 30 days.

Children

Arzi is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
